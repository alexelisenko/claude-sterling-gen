## Assembly sequence (per side, working outward from the cold head)

1. Press/braze the cooler insert (SG-203) into the cold head Ø94 bore; weld the water-gallery sleeve; pressure-test the gallery.
2. Displacer cylinder (SG-209) spigots into the cold head Ø68 bore against the shoulder (slip fit; clamped for good once the hull studs are torqued).
3. Slide the regenerator matrix (SG-202) over the displacer cylinder, seated against the cold head face.
4. Displacer (SG-206) with rod goes into the displacer cylinder from the hot end.
5. Hot cap (SG-201) slides over the whole stack, o-ring on the cold head inboard face, 8 x M8 BCD 140.
6. Cold side: hull onto the cold head studs, o-ring, torque. Stator core + potted coil (SG-211/212) slide into the hull bore from the outboard end, seat against the integral stop ring (leads out through the core OD slot).
7. Piston + magnet ring loads through the stator bore (Ø120 ring through Ø122 core, guided); displacer rod enters its piston bushing.
8. Bounce cap: spigot clamps the stator through a wave spring; feedthrough + charge valve; 8 x M8 BCD 200.
9. Into the firebox (INSERTION PATH VERIFIED by swept-collision check): pedestals top below the Ø200 flange sweep, so each bench-assembled side slides in horizontally, dome-first, fins passing through the 160x150 wall aperture. Then: saddle blocks (SG-219) drop onto the pedestals under the necks; split panels (SG-220, two halves) clamp around the bare Ø88 fire tube and bolt over the aperture; ceramic rope packs the Ø92 bore.
